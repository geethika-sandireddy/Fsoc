Problem Statement 26169 Overview

Free Space Optical Communication (FSOC) relies on pointing, acquisition, and tracking (PAT) of highly narrow laser beams. Coarse alignment acquires the remote beacon within the camera Field of View (FOV) and adjusts pan-tilt orientation before fine pointing engages.
